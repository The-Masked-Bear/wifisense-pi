WiFi Sense
Sponsor Project

See through walls with ordinary WiFi.

An ESP32-S3 and a Raspberry Pi detect motion, presence, and breathing through solid walls using Channel State Information.

View on GitHub ❤️ Support the Creator
🌊

Motion & Presence

Tells an empty room from a room with a motionless person in it. Something PIR sensors constantly fail at.

🫁

Breathing Rate

Detects a chest moving just 6mm. Tracks respiration from 8 to 36 breaths per minute with incredible accuracy.

🧱

Through Solid Walls

2.4 GHz passes right through plasterboard, wood, and glass. Monitor a room without ever stepping foot inside.
